# campaigns/ — rules for this folder

These rules apply to everything inside `campaigns/`, on top of the studio-wide
rules in the root `CLAUDE.md`.

**One folder per campaign.** Keep that campaign's plan, emails, groups, and
results together. Do not mix assets between campaigns.

**Email files follow `.claude/rules/emails.md`.** That file controls subject and
preview length, greeting, links, sign-off, and footer.

**Plans and copy follow `brand/voice.md`.** Check banned words, claims, and
urgency against it.

**Use practice leads only.** Do not add real people or real addresses. See
`leads/CLAUDE.md`.
