# Kettlebird Tea — Brand Voice

Kettlebird Tea is a small online tea shop. Its monthly subscription box is called
**The Steep Club**, and the people we write to joined its waitlist.

Our audience is students learning to become forward deployed engineers — people who
spend their days inside unfamiliar codebases and half-finished problems. They are
short on time, short on money, and allergic to being talked down to. Everything
below follows from that.

---

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Calm. Precise. Wry.**

- **Calm** — no urgency, no countdowns, no "don't miss out". We are the steady part
  of their week, not another thing demanding attention.
- **Precise** — say the one true thing. Name the tea, the price, the date. If a
  sentence could be shorter or more specific, it should be.
- **Wry** — one dry line, when it's earned. Never a joke that needs explaining,
  never a pun in a subject line.

Warm underneath all three: we are on their side. Warmth shows up as being useful,
not as exclamation marks.

## 2. Words we use

- Plain, everyday words: cup, box, tea, month, price, post.
- **"You"** and **"we"**. Never "our valued subscribers" or "the customer".
- Concrete names for real things: "this month's tea", "the Steep Club box", "your
  first box".
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Useful specifics: how long to steep it, what it tastes like, when it arrives.
- Direct instructions: "Steep it for three minutes." "Reply to this email if it's
  wrong."

## 3. Words we avoid

- **Hype:** game-changing, revolutionary, elevate, unlock, seamless, next-level,
  premium experience, must-have.
- **Startup filler:** journey, curated, obsessed, passionate, disrupt, ecosystem,
  leverage, space.
- **Hustle talk:** grind, 10x, level up, crush it, no days off, productivity hack.
- **Fake friendliness:** guys, bestie, tea lovers!, superfans.
- **False urgency:** only 3 left, last chance, hurry, don't miss out.
- **Punctuation:** no exclamation marks, no ALL CAPS, no emoji.
- **Health-adjacent words:** detox, cleanse, superfood, wellness, immunity,
  focus-boosting.

## 4. Claims we must never make

- **No health claims.** Nothing about curing, treating, preventing or improving any
  condition. No "boosts immunity", "improves focus", "aids sleep", "helps you
  concentrate", "supports weight loss", "reduces stress". A cup of tea is a nice
  drink, not a treatment.
- **No "clinically proven" or "scientifically formulated"** about our tea, ever.
- **No certifications we do not hold.** Organic, Fairtrade, vegan, gluten-free —
  only if that specific tea is actually certified, and then only with the certifier
  named.
- **No environmental promises** such as carbon neutral, plastic free or 100%
  compostable unless we can point to the paperwork.
- **No invented scarcity or social proof.** No "our most popular box" or "hundreds
  of students love it" unless it is true and current.
- **No guarantees about outcomes.** We can promise the tea tastes good and arrives
  when we say. We cannot promise it will make anyone a better engineer.

If a claim feels like it needs a footnote, cut it.

## 5. How we sign off

Every email ends with one short closing line, then the signature.

- **Marketing and newsletter emails:** `Talk soon,` then `— Kettlebird`
- **Shipping and account emails:** no closing line, just `— Kettlebird` and the
  support address.
- **Never** sign as a made-up person. If we ever want a named human at the bottom,
  it has to be a real one.
- **Never** use "Cheers", "Best regards", "Warm wishes", or an emoji as a
  signature.
