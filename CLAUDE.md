# Email Marketing Studio

This folder is a workspace for planning and writing email marketing campaigns for
a small business. The work here is mostly writing and thinking: campaign plans,
email sequences, subject lines, and the copy that goes inside them. Some of it
will eventually be built into a sending setup, but the studio itself is a place to
produce the words and the plan first.

## Folders

- **brand/** — who the business is: voice, tone, positioning, audience notes, and anything that keeps the writing sounding like them.
- **leads/** — information about the people we write to: lists, segments, where they came from, and what we know about them.
- **campaigns/** — one folder per campaign, holding the plan, the emails, and any variants or results.
- **notes/** — research, decisions, and reference material that is not itself a campaign or a brand asset.

## Rules

**Write in plain English.** Short sentences, everyday words. No jargon, no
corporate filler, no "leverage" or "synergy". If a sentence sounds like it came
from a press release, rewrite it. This applies to the campaigns we write for the
client too, not just to notes for ourselves.

**Never send email to real people.** Nothing in this studio sends to a live
audience. Do not connect a real sending account, do not import a real customer
list, and do not run any command that would deliver mail to an actual inbox. If a
test send is needed, it goes to a dedicated test address that you have approved
first, and only after asking. Writing the email is the job; sending it is not.

**Ask before deleting anything.** No deleting files, folders, campaigns, or
content without checking first. If something looks like it should go, say what it
is and why, then wait for an answer. This includes overwriting a file with a
shorter version, which loses the old content just as surely as deleting it.
